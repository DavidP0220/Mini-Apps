**Tres conclusiones que cambian el plan:**

1. **La fórmula C rinde 6,4x más que la A en su mejor caso** (864k vs 134k), desde canales de tamaño
   casi idéntico (3.920 vs 3.180 subs). El plan alterna ambas, pero abre con C.
2. **La duración no es la variable.** El breakout de 864k dura 7:23; el de 62k dura 30:54. Lo que
   separa es la **especificidad del arquetipo**, no los minutos. Se mantiene el objetivo de 10-13 min
   del playbook porque reduce a la mitad las vistas necesarias para las 4.000 horas, no porque suba
   las vistas.
3. **Canales de 2.400-4.000 subs están haciendo 60k-864k vistas por video.** Mindset Mechanics tiene
   11 subs y mejor conversión que todos ellos. El techo no es el canal, es el volumen de vistas.

## 4. Reglas transversales (aplican a los 8)

- **Duración:** 10-13 min. Se llega añadiendo 1-2 actos más de la misma plantilla, no alargando actos.
- **Cero intro, cero logo, cero música de entrada, cero "hey guys".** Empieza en frío en el fotograma 1.
- **La palabra del título NUNCA aparece en los primeros 15-22 segundos.** Regla verificada en 4 canales.
- **Cadencia de guion:** 150-172 palabras/minuto, sin relleno.
- **Ancla dura** (número exacto o nombre propio) cada 20-30 segundos.
- **Nunca pedir la suscripción hablada.** La única línea de suscripción va en la descripción, al final,
  con `?sub_confirmation=1`, en imperativo con el nombre del canal como objeto.
- **CTA de comentario-número** en un límite de sección al 40% y al 85-90%. Nunca en los primeros 3 min.
- **Miniatura, plantilla ONLI:** fondo crema plano, host a la derecha (gorra navy + hoodie gris,
  45-50% de la altura), globo amarillo `#FFE600` a la izquierda con 2-3 palabras bicolor
  (negro = contexto, rojo = palabra emocional), flecha curva a mano apuntando a la cabeza.
  **Cero solapamiento léxico con el título.**
- **Estilo visual:** `ESTILO_MINDSET_MECHANICS.md` plantilla v4, con `REFERENCIA_personaje.png`
  cargado vía `import_local_image()` + "Use Consistent Character".
- **Salida 1920x1080 mínimo**, subtítulos quemados obligatorios (de ellos dependen los Shorts).
- **Marcar siempre "Uso de IA: Sí"** y "No creado para niños".

### #1 · 29 ago · Why Your Brain Breaks Under Pressure And How to Stop It
**Score 98 · 10-13 min · REGENERAR desde `script_resilience_video.md` + `RESILIENCE_SCENE_PLAN.md`**

Objeto concreto: el bloqueo mental en una reunión, delante de gente.
El guion y las 12 escenas ya existen. Lo que hay que arreglar es la ejecución: estilo cómic
equivocado, personaje inconsistente, sin subtítulos, 720p, letreros con texto basura generado por IA.

Conceptos con nombre: **indefensión aprendida** (Seligman, 1967), **carga alostática** (McEwen, 1993),
**suspiro fisiológico**.

**Hook (0-30s):**
> Everyone in the room is waiting for you to speak. You know this. You prepared for this. You have
> said these exact words out loud, alone, maybe thirty times. And now there is nothing. Not fear
> exactly. Just a blank white space where the sentence used to be.
> That is not a personality flaw. That is a system doing its job.
> It was built for a moment when going quiet and going still was the difference between being seen
> and not being seen. Between eaten and not eaten.
> The problem is that it cannot tell the difference between a predator and a project deadline.

CTA al 40%: *"If your mind has ever gone blank in front of people, comment one. If it happens to you
when you're alone too, comment zero."*

### #2 · 2 sep · The Psychology of People Who Can't Sit in Silence
**Score 96 · fórmula C · 10-13 min**

Objeto concreto: poner música al entrar al coche antes de arrancar; un podcast en la ducha; la TV de
fondo en una casa vacía.

Conceptos con nombre: **tarea de vigilancia de Mackworth** (1948), **detección hiperactiva de agencia
/ HADD** (Barrett, 2000), **efecto Zeigarnik** (1927).

Ángulo evolutivo: en la sabana el silencio no era paz — era la señal de que los pájaros habían dejado
de cantar. El silencio significaba que algo se acercaba. Un cerebro que se relajaba en silencio no
dejó descendencia.

**Hook (0-30s):**
> You get in the car. Before you put on your seatbelt, before you even start the engine, your hand
> goes to the radio. You are not choosing a song. You are filling something.
> Every human alive today descends from someone who survived long enough to have children. That is
> the only filter evolution ever ran.
> And for three hundred thousand years, the animals that lived were the ones who noticed when the
> forest went quiet. The birds stop. The insects stop. Something is moving.
> Silence was never rest. Silence was the last warning you got.

Bucles abiertos: *"But that creates another problem."* / *"It's an elegant theory. And once again, it
isn't proven."* / *"Then modern life complicated everything even further."*

Giro meta (~70%): *"Maybe we have been asking the wrong question. The question isn't why you can't
sit in silence. It's why you think you should be able to."*

Cierre citable: *"You are not bad at being alone with your thoughts. You are very good at listening
for something that stopped coming three hundred thousand years ago."*

### #3 · 6 sep · Why Didn't Evolution Fix Your Attention Span?
**Score 95 · fórmula A · 10-13 min**

Objeto concreto: el móvil boca abajo en la mesa que igual revisas.
Conecta directo con el Short ya publicado *"Your Brain Can't Tell a Lion From a Slack Notification"*
— enlazarlo como Related Video.

Conceptos con nombre: **descuento hiperbólico** (Ainslie, 1975), **principio del detector de humo**
(Nesse, 2001), **vigilancia de Mackworth** (1948).

**Hook (0-30s):**
> You put the phone face down. That was a decision. You made it on purpose. Ninety seconds later your
> hand is already turning it back over and you do not remember deciding that part.
> Every animal has a limited budget of attention, and evolution spends it the same way every time: on
> whatever might change your odds of surviving the next ten minutes.
> A rustle in the grass could be wind. It could also be the last sound you ever hear.
> Being wrong about the wind costs you nothing. Being wrong about the leopard costs you everything.
> So the system was built to be wrong constantly, in one specific direction.

Giro meta (~70%): *"Your attention span was never broken. It was calibrated for a world where
everything that grabbed it was rare."*

### #5 · 14 sep · Why Didn't Evolution Remove Procrastination?
**Score 94 · fórmula A · 10-13 min**

Objeto concreto: la tarea concreta que llevas tres semanas sin hacer y que tomaría cuarenta minutos.

Conceptos con nombre: **descuento hiperbólico** (Ainslie, 1975), **efecto Zeigarnik** (1927),
**presupuesto energético / forrajeo óptimo**.

**Hook (0-30s):**
> There is a thing you have not done. You know exactly what it is. It would take about forty minutes.
> You have now spent three weeks not doing it, which is roughly thirty times longer than doing it.
> For almost all of human history, calories were the currency of everything. Not money. Calories.
> And a body that spent calories on a task with no immediate return was a body making a bet it could
> not afford to lose.
> Effort now, reward later, was not wisdom. It was a way to die in a bad winter.

Giro meta (~70%): *"Maybe people have been asking the wrong question. You are not avoiding the task.
You are avoiding the feeling the task predicts."*

### #6 · 18 sep · The Psychology of People Who Feel Watched in an Empty Room
**Score 92 · fórmula C · 10-13 min**

Objeto concreto: girarte en tu propia casa vacía porque juraste que alguien te miraba.

Conceptos con nombre: **detección hiperactiva de agencia / HADD** (Barrett, 2000), **efecto foco**
(Gilovich, 2000), **detección de mirada**.

**Hook (0-30s):**
> You are alone in your own home. You have been alone for hours. And then, for no reason you can point
> to, you turn around.
> There is a piece of your brain whose entire job is to answer one question, constantly, without ever
> asking your permission: is something looking at me right now?
> It runs in the dark. It runs when you are reading. It runs when you are asleep.
> And it was built by a filter with no interest in whether it is right — only in what it costs to be
> wrong.

Cierre citable: *"The room was always empty. The system that checks was never built to believe you."*

### #7 · 22 sep · The Psychology of People Who Rehearse Conversations in the Shower
**Score 91 · fórmula C · 10-13 min**

Conceptos con nombre: **simulación mental / red neuronal por defecto**, **número de Dunbar** (Dunbar,

> That is not daydreaming. That is a simulator, and it was expensive to build.
> Something that costs that much energy does not survive three hundred thousand years unless it is

CTA al 85%: *"If you rehearse conversations that never happen, comment one. If you replay ones that

### #8 · 25 sep · The Psychology of People Who Apologize for Everything
**Score 91 · fórmula C · 10-13 min**

Objeto concreto: decir "perdón" cuando alguien choca contigo.

Conceptos con nombre: **exhibiciones de apaciguamiento** (etología), **número de Dunbar**,
**coste de la exclusión del grupo**.

**Hook (0-30s):**
> Someone walks into you. They were not looking. You were standing still. And you are the one who says

> For most of human history, there was no such thing as surviving alone. Not as a hardship. As a
> category that did not exist.
> The group had the fire, the food, the warning, the water. Outside the group there was no version of

> So the most dangerous thing a human could do was not lose a fight. It was become someone the group
> stopped protecting.

Giro meta (~70%): *"Maybe the question isn't why you apologize so much. It's what you think happens
if you stop."*

## 6. Shorts derivados (decisión híbrida)

**No se produce ningún Short desde cero.** Todos salen de recortar el largo con `make_ultrashorts.sh`,
coste marginal casi cero. 3 por video largo → **24 Shorts en el mes**.

Reglas derivadas del A/B ya resuelto en el canal:

- **12-18 segundos.** El corte de 18s hizo 84 vistas; el de 31s del mismo material hizo 12.
- **Un solo hecho concreto y visceral** por Short.
- **Cero jerga clínica en la frase de apertura.** Nada de "amígdala", "cortisol", "ganglios basales"
  como primera palabra. El Short con jerga se quedó en 15% de retención y el algoritmo lo mató.
- **Badge de SUBSCRIBE quemado** en los últimos 3,5s (`make_shorts_badge.py` + `add_badge.sh`).
  Los Shorts anteriores hicieron 95 vistas y 0 subs porque nunca se pedía nada al final.
- **Enlazar cada Short a su video largo** como Related Video. Es el único mecanismo que convierte
  vistas de Shorts en horas de reproducción. La verificación del canal se aprobó el 19-ago —
  confirmar que ya funciona.
- **1080x1920 mínimo**, sin excepción.
- Cortar en fronteras de silencio: `silencedetect=noise=-30dB:d=0.25`.

## 8. Lo que este plan NO resuelve

Ninguno de estos 8 videos monetiza el canal en 30 días. Faltan 989 suscriptores ≈ 36.000 vistas de
largos. El plan busca **el video que rompe**, porque la ley de potencia del nicho es que 3-10% de los
videos generan 75-90% de las vistas — y con 8 tiros bien apuntados la probabilidad deja de ser
despreciable.

La única vía de ingreso real dentro de la ventana de 30 días sigue siendo el infoproducto en inglés
con el motor de Mini-Apps, link en descripción y en "Acerca de". No depende de ningún umbral.
